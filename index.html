01 — Services

What we fabricate

Mild steel, stainless and aluminium, cut, welded and finished in our own workshop. Everything is made to measure — we do not fit off-the-shelf kits.

Staircases

Straight flights, external steps and fire escape stairs, fabricated to your opening and landing heights. Treads in chequer plate, open grating, or left ready for timber.

Internal / external

Railings & handrails

Stair railings, garden and step handrails, and continuous rails around landings. Made to fit uneven ground, stone steps and existing brickwork.

Domestic / commercial

Balustrades

Vertical bar, flat bar and infill panel balustrades for balconies, mezzanines and stairwells, set out to the guarding heights your building control officer will ask for.

Guarding

Fire escapes

External escape staircases, walkways and platforms, galvanised or powder coated for a long life outdoors. Surveyed, fabricated and installed by the same team.

Escape routes

Structural steel

Beams, posts, frames and support steelwork for extensions, mezzanines and larger commercial projects. Delivered to site and fitted to your engineer's drawings.

Large scale

Bespoke & repairs

Gates, security grilles, frames, brackets and one-off metalwork. We also repair and re-weld existing steel rather than replacing it, where that makes better sense.

One-offs

02 — Process

How a job runs

Four stages from the first call to the steel being bolted down. You will know the price before anything is cut.

STAGE 01

Site visit

We come out, take the measurements ourselves and talk through what will actually work in the space. No charge for this.

STAGE 02

Drawing & price

You get a drawing and a fixed written quotation covering steel, finish, delivery and fitting. Nothing gets added later.

STAGE 03

Fabrication

Cut, welded and dressed in the workshop, then galvanised or powder coated to the colour you have chosen.

STAGE 04

Installation

Our own fitters install it, check it is solid and clear up after themselves. We keep you posted at every stage.
